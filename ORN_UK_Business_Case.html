The Opportunity · 2027

A serious UK operation — Europe's delivery engine.

For ORN to win in Europe, it needs a real European office — not a nominal one. A London-based operational DMC that contracts product, controls quality, delivers every booking, and owns the member-club credit programme. This is the only way the UK and Europe will work now.

Quick scenarios:

🇪🇺Europe is the prize

High-value inbound from India, the Middle East, Southeast Asia and Australia — all flowing into European product. The margin is real; today it leaks through poor booking and no control on the ground.

🏛️Mayfair base

17 Hanover Square — best-in-class London address. A credible operational HQ that partners, hotels and airlines take seriously, not a back office.

🎫Member clubs & credits

A recurring revenue line beyond servicing — white-label membership travel clubs distributing member credits, built on our own platform.

Why now

The cost of doing nothing

Europe has been funded for years with no delivery to standard. Bookings are mishandled, product is wrongly loaded, and margin that belongs to the group is lost. Every month without a real operation is money left on the table.

The 2027 window

With licensing in place, a funded team and an agreed marketing budget, we move from fixing to selling. Set up now and 2027 is a full trading year as a licensed UK operation — not a standing start.

The global operating model

ORN Europe is the hub. Sales and tech for inbound sit close to each source market — but delivery, quality and control run through London.

🇮🇳India — Bangalore

Inbound sales & technology for the India market. ORN Europe services, quality-controls and delivers every booking — and sells once licensed.

🇦🇺Oceania — Australia

Inbound sales for Australia & New Zealand. Europe owns reconfirmation, QC and ground delivery, with follow-the-sun cover.

🇬🇧UK & Europe — London

The operational hub: contracting, QC, delivery, product, member clubs — and direct European sales once licensed. The engine for all offices.
