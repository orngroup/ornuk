The Opportunity · £10m Year 1

Build in the UK what we proved in Australia.

The ORN Australia operation has been a success — selling globally from one operational base. We now need the same in the UK: an office that sells Europe and worldwide, and acts as the DMC, quality-control and delivery engine for European travel across the whole group. We are not starting cold — the UK team already has the experience, the live sales and the supplier and partner contacts, and we are IN Europe, on the doorstep of the product.

Australia proved the model. The UK, sitting inside Europe with an experienced team and contacts already in place, is the natural hub to contract, control and deliver European product — for every ORN office and in its own right. The target: £10m of sales in year one.

The product mix we sell into it

🚌Tours & escorted touring

One of the fastest-recovering, highest-margin segments — multi-country itineraries through France, Switzerland, Italy. Exactly what an in-Europe DMC contracts and delivers best.

🚐Private van & small-group tours

Premium, bespoke private touring — the Sacred Journeys model — commands £2,000+ per booking, highest daily spend, and travels well across member clubs.

🏙️City breaks

The volume engine — Paris, Barcelona, Madrid, Rome, Amsterdam. Short, repeatable, broad appeal; feeds both direct sales and the member-club credit programme.

🛳️Cruise, river & experiences

Rhine and European river cruise, plus experiences — contracted at source, delivered to the group's one standard.

Why do this — and why now

The market is already here

70.6m UK trips to Europe every year, growing 15% over five years. We don't need to create demand — we need to capture a sliver of an established, growing flow we sit right inside.

We're positioned, not starting cold

Experienced team, live sales, supplier and partner contacts, and 5.5m member reach through the credit programme — the pieces are in place now, not in two years.

The cost of waiting

Every month without control on the ground, margin leaks through poor booking and errors. Australia proved the model; delay just hands the European upside to someone else.

⚠ Working capital as sales scale

The set-up and running costs above are held flat — as agreed, we are not inflating the cost base. But £10m of sales means real cash moving: deposits to suppliers often go out before client monies clear, so as bookings ramp from spring, additional short-term working capital may be needed to bridge supplier payments. This is funding against secured, paid-for bookings — not operating loss — and would be drawn only as volume requires.
