The Opportunity · 2027

Build in the UK what we proved in Australia.

The ORN Australia operation has been a success — selling globally from one operational base. We now need the same in the UK: an office that sells Europe and worldwide, and acts as the DMC, quality-control and delivery engine for European travel across the whole group.

Australia proved the model. The UK, sitting inside Europe, is the natural hub to contract, control and deliver European product — for every ORN office and in its own right.

The Australia → UK conversion

Australia built a profitable global-selling operation from scratch. The UK has a structural advantage Australia didn't: it sits on Europe's doorstep, the destination the whole group is selling. That shortens the path from set-up to revenue — the UK can convert fast because the product, the suppliers and the demand are all here.

Why now

Proven, not speculative

This isn't a bet — it's replicating a model that already works in Australia, in the one market closest to the product. The risk is in not doing it: margin keeps leaking through poor booking and no control on the ground.

A full trading year in 2027

Licensed, funded and staffed now, 2027 is a complete trading year. By year-end the operation is profitable — the investment funds the ramp, not an ongoing loss.
